School English dictation again? Parents shouldn’t have to read the list aloud every night. FunFunSpell lets you build the practice, listen, spell, and fix weak words anywhere.

Great for:
• School English dictation / listen-and-spell practice
• Exam revision for textbook words and sentences
• Drilling words you keep misspelling

How it works (four beats)
1. Create your list — word cards or full sentences that match class content
2. Listen — AI voice reads any English word or sentence
3. Spell — practice the dictation, repeat as needed
4. Weak words — track learned words and ones you often misspell

Why FunFunSpell
• Free forever — all core features free; no ads, no subscription
• Unlimited custom dictations you can save and reuse
• Images and phonetic symbols in single-word practice
• 4,000+ commonly used English words included
• Web version at funfunspell.com

About the developer
FunFunSpell is an indie project maintained in free time. It stays free because it’s a personal learning tool—not a subscription product.
